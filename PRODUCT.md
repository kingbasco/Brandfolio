# Brandfolio product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Implemented: React/TypeScript/Vite frontend for the public landing page. Planned continuation: Cloudflare Workers, hosted Supabase PostgreSQL and Auth, Cloudflare R2, hosted processing and AI. Development takes place in Codespaces or another cloud workspace. No backend service or public deployment is configured by the landing-page implementation.

## Users

Primary: freelance brand designers and small studios documenting existing identities for clients. Secondary: recipients of published guidelines, future team members, template contributors and platform administrators. These are product hypotheses grounded in the owner’s brief, not validated research personas.

## Product Purpose

Reduce repetitive documentation and layout work while preserving designer control. A designer supplies identity assets and approved information, chooses a template, edits the result, and delivers a consistent guideline.

## Positioning

Editable structured brand documentation with reusable template bindings and assisted import. No exclusive market or performance claims are established.

## Operating Context

Designers work with logo variants, palette values, fonts, written rules, images and existing PDF brand books. Desktop is the primary authoring context. Mobile supports account tasks, browsing and reading; editor controls must remain operable without implying mobile feature parity that has not been tested.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Available now: a public landing page and catalog of 12 original cover concepts, with search, style filters, sorting, load more, preview and SVG cover download. Covers are labeled as concepts, not complete editable guideline templates.

Planned product capabilities and constraints:

- Brand workspaces, asset libraries and guidelines.
- Versioned templates with brand-field bindings; switching presentation preserves content.
- Editable sections, revisions, reliable saving and recovery.
- Hosted PDF export and revision-bound sharing.
- PDF/image extraction and assisted reconstruction; exact reproduction is not promised.
- Optional AI drafting and suggestions; existing hosted models first, no owner-computer inference.
- Private templates and later reviewed community submissions.
- Administrative user, template, job, usage and moderation functions.
- Optional permissioned AI improvement later; upload never implies public sharing or training permission.
- Free-first, not unlimited-free. Costly actions require enforced limits and provider verification.

## Brand Commitments

Working name: Brandfolio. The owner rejected the prior interface and selected an expressive creative-studio direction. The owner subsequently chose TapTap as the design-system source. The current repository preserves product goals while adopting its visual foundations.

## Evidence on Hand

Conversation requirements, design-system artifacts, a working S01 frontend, original fictional SVG covers, and scoped browser/build verification. No production customers, measured time savings, proprietary design assets, trained model or legal approvals. Any specimen content must be labeled fictional/sample.

## Product Principles

1. Designers approve creative and factual decisions.
2. Documents, templates and source assets have clear ownership and versioning.
3. Private data remains private until an explicit, scoped sharing action.
4. A dependable end-to-end workflow matters more than feature count.
5. AI failure must not prevent manual work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Project target: WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard access, visible focus, non-drag alternatives, readable forms, reduced motion and error recovery are baseline requirements. English first; externalize UI strings and use locale-aware formatting. No Japan-market-specific scope has been established.

## Success measures

Measure time to first completed guideline, editing correction effort, successful exports, revision recovery, template reuse and cost per completed document. Establish baselines in pilot testing rather than inventing targets or results.
